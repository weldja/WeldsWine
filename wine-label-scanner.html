What the wine scanner reads from a label

Wine labels pack a lot into a small space, often in another language. The scanner uses AI to read both the front and back label and pull out the details that matter, so your journal entry is done in seconds.

Wine name and producer
The château, domaine, estate or brand, and the name of the wine itself.
Region and country
Where it comes from, so your wines can be shown on a map of the world.
Grape
The grape or blend, which is often only printed on the back label.
Vintage
The year on the bottle, so you can tell one year from another.
Tasting notes
Where the label describes the wine, those notes are filled in for you to keep or replace with your own.
Your photo of the label
Kept with the wine, so you can spot the same bottle on the shelf next time.

How to scan a wine label

  1. Photograph the label

    Take a photo of the front label, then the back. You can use your phone’s camera or a photo you already have.

  2. Check what it read

    The details are filled in for you. Have a quick look, change anything that isn’t right, and add your score out of 10.

  3. It’s in your journal

    Save, and the wine joins your journal. Search it, sort by score or grape, see it on the map, or share it with a friend.

Tips for a good scan

  • Fill the frame with the label, and hold the phone straight on rather than at an angle.
  • Avoid glare: tilt the bottle away from bright lights or a window.
  • On a curved bottle, keep the middle of the label facing you; the edges matter less.
  • Always add the back label. It is usually where the grape, region and alcohol are printed.

Questions

Is the wine scanner free?

Yes. You can try it straight away without signing up. Create a free account when you want to keep your journal, and anything you saved while trying it comes with you.

What does it read from the label?

The wine’s name, the producer, the region, the grape and the vintage, plus tasting notes where the label has them. Photograph the back label too: that is often where the grape and region are printed.

Do I need to download an app?

No. It works in your phone’s web browser. If you use it often, you can install it from your browser so it opens from your home screen like any other app.

What devices does it work on?

Any device with a camera: iPhone, Android, iPad, laptop or desktop, in any modern web browser.

What if it reads something wrong?

Nothing is saved until you have looked at it. Every detail it fills in can be changed before you save, and you can edit a wine later too. Glare, curved labels and fancy lettering are the usual causes of a misread.

How is this different from the Shelf Scanner?

The wine label scanner is for one bottle: it reads the label and adds that wine to your journal. The Shelf Scanner is for a whole shop shelf: it reads every wine and price tag and tells you which bottles are the best value.

Can I share wines with friends?

Yes. You can share a single wine recommendation, or join a shared cellar with friends to see each other’s finds, ratings, comments and tasting events.
